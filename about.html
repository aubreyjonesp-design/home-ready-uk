About Home Ready UK

Independent

We are independent. We are not part of the UK Government, the British Red Cross, any charity, or any energy or water company. We are not paid by them. We use no Red Cross logos or emblems.

What we believe

  • No alarmism. Most emergencies are short. We don’t use countdowns, scary pictures or “disaster” language.
  • Official advice first. We follow GOV.UK Prepare and link to it. If we differ, the official advice wins.
  • Low cost, low waste. Build up slowly. Use what you already eat and drink, and use the oldest first.
  • For everyone. Plain English, large print, easy to print, and usable with a keyboard or screen reader.

How we source and check

  • Every guide lists its sources and a “last checked” date.
  • We use official and expert sources: GOV.UK, energy networks (105), water companies, the Food Standards Agency and the British Red Cross.
  • We never make up figures. Survey numbers come only from the published report, with a link.
  • We aim to recheck every page at least every 6 months, and soon after official advice changes.
  • We write in our own words. Where we use GOV.UK text, it is Crown copyright, used under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
  • Spotted a mistake? Email homereadyuk@mail.grokbot.com.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies, no advertising trackers and no outside fonts. We count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and stores no IP addresses. The calculator runs in your browser and saves nothing. We never ask for health details.

Affiliate and advertising policy

We have no affiliate links and no adverts right now. If we ever add them, each one will be clearly labelled “Ad”, and they will never appear inside the core safety advice. Following our advice never requires buying anything from us.
