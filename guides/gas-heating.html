No gas or heating: what to do

Last checked: 15 October 2026

If you smell gas or think there is a leak, call 0800 111 999 (free, 24 hours, Great Britain). Open doors and windows, do not use light switches or flames, and turn off the gas at the meter if you can. In Northern Ireland, use the gas emergency number on your gas bill.

Get ready now

  • Have your boiler and gas appliances checked by a Gas Safe registered engineer.
  • Fit a carbon monoxide alarm that meets British or European standards (look for BS Kitemark or EN 50291).
  • Know where your gas shut-off is. Write it on your plan.
  • Keep warm layers, blankets and hot water bottles handy.
  • Keep enough medicine and food in case it is hard to get out in cold weather.
  • If you or someone you care for would struggle without heat, join the Priority Services Register.

If your heating stops

  • Check whether it is just you. If your power is also off, it may be a power cut (call 105). Most boilers need electricity.
  • Stay in one room and keep the door shut. Wear layers, a hat and socks. Have warm drinks.
  • Never use a gas cooker, barbecue or camping heater to heat your home.
  • Check on older people, babies and anyone unwell. They feel the cold most.
  • Know the signs of being too cold. Call 111 for advice, or 999 in an emergency.
