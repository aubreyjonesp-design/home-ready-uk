Be Ready 72-hour checklist (UK): what to keep for 3 days

Last checked: 23 October 2026

On 22 October 2026 the UK Government launched its Be Ready campaign. It asks households to be ready to manage for 3 days (72 hours) if services like power, water, phones or the internet are disrupted. Here is the official checklist, line by line, worked out for your household.

Why 3 days

The Government says being ready for 3 days “helps you manage while support reaches those most in need”. It mentions severe weather and cyber attacks as examples of things that can disrupt everyday services. Most disruptions are short, and you do not need to do it all at once.

How to get started (from the official advice)

  • See what you already have. Check your cupboards first.
  • Plan for your household. Note what you still need, including medical supplies, baby formula or pet food.
  • Build it gradually. Pick up an extra item or two on your normal shop to spread the cost.
  • Check it regularly. Set a reminder every 6 months to check dates and swap items.
  • Connect with your community. Talk to neighbours and swap contact details.

The checklist, with amounts

The items come from the official Be Ready checklist. The water amounts use the official 3 litres per person per day. The other amounts are our own suggestions, not official figures, and food is shown as 3 meals a day.

Free things you can do today

  • Sign up to the Priority Services Register if you or someone at home may need extra help.
  • Write down important numbers and fill in our one-page household plan (large print).
  • Find your water stop tap, fuse box and gas shut-off.
  • Check what you already have against the emergency kit list, and think about a grab bag in case you need to leave home.
