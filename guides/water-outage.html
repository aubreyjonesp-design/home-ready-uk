No water: what to do

Last checked: 15 October 2026

Water cuts are usually fixed quickly. Your water company is the best place for news if it lasts longer.

Get ready now

  • Keep some sealed bottled water. GOV.UK says at least 2.5 to 3 litres per person per day to drink, and 10 litres per person per day to be more comfortable. Use our calculator to work out your amount.
  • GOV.UK suggests buying bottled water rather than filling your own containers.
  • Find out who your water company is. Water UK: find your supplier.
  • Know where your stop tap is, so you can turn the water off if a pipe leaks.
  • If someone needs extra water for health reasons, or uses medical equipment that needs water, join your water company’s Priority Services Register and talk to their care team about a plan.
  • Rain butt water is fine for flushing the toilet, but do not drink it.

During a water outage

  • Check your water company’s website or social media for updates and where to collect bottled water.
  • If you are on the Priority Services Register, your water company should arrange water for you. If it doesn’t arrive, contact them.
  • Save bottled water for drinking, cooking, washing hands and making baby formula.
  • Use hand sanitiser and wet wipes to stay clean.
  • Check on neighbours who may struggle to collect water.
