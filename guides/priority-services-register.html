The Priority Services Register

Last checked: 15 October 2026

A free service from energy and water companies for people who need extra help, especially when supplies are cut off.

Who it is for

You may be able to join if, for example, you are of pensionable age, disabled, have a long-term health condition, rely on medical equipment, have young children, or need extra help for a while (such as after an operation). Check the exact rules at thepsr.co.uk.

What you might get

  • Advance warning of planned power or water cuts.
  • Priority support in a power cut, or bottled water delivered in a water outage.
  • Bills and letters in large print, braille or other formats, and a password scheme so you know a caller is genuine.

How to join

  • Contact your energy supplier (the company you pay bills to). Not sure who it is? Look at a bill, or use Find My Supplier.
  • Contact your water company too. They keep a separate register. Find your water company.
  • Your electricity network operator (the company you call on 105) can also add you.
  • Caring for someone? Ask if they are registered, and help them sign up.
  • In Scotland, see PSR Scotland. In Northern Ireland these are called Customer Care Registers.
