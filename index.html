Why we made this

In a 2026 UK Government survey, half of adults (50%) said they felt only slightly or not at all prepared for an emergency. Water was the biggest gap: only 4% had 3 or more days of bottled water at home. The most common reasons people gave were cost, not wanting things to go out of date, and not knowing what to do.[1]

So we keep it simple, low cost and low waste. We point you to the official advice, and help you keep it up over time.

For older relatives and carers

Everything here prints clearly and has a large-print version. You can fill in a plan for someone you look after, and check whether they could get free extra help from their energy and water companies through the Priority Services Register.
